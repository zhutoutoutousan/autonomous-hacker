3. **Authorization**: Only use this tool:

   - In compliance with applicable laws

## Prohibited Uses

**DO NOT** use this tool to:
- Harass, stalk, or intimidate individuals
- Violate platform Terms of Service
- Collect data without authorization
- Engage in illegal activities

## Ethical Guidelines

1. **Respect Privacy**: Only gather publicly available information
2. **Minimize Impact**: Use rate limiting and respect platform resources
3. **Purpose Limitation**: Use gathered intelligence only for stated defensive purposes
4. **Data Retention**: Securely store and delete data when no longer needed
5. **Transparency**: Be transparent about your use of OSINT tools when required
